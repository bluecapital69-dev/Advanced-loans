Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

1. Our commitment

MTN Advance respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

When you apply for an advance, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Phone number and MTN MoMo number
  • National ID number
  • Application details (amount, period)
  • Device and browser information for security

3. How we use your information

  • To verify your identity and process your application
  • To send you SMS verification codes
  • To disburse funds to your MoMo wallet
  • To communicate with you about your advance
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with:

  • MTN MoMo to process the transaction
  • Regulatory authorities when legally required
  • Service providers who help us operate the platform

5. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure, and you should keep your MoMo PIN private at all times.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of your personal information
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information (subject to legal requirements)

7. Contact us

For any privacy questions, contact us at privacy@mtnadvance.cm.
